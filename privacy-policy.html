Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Velo Growth ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our automated missed-call text-back service, used on behalf of client businesses (such as med spas and other local service providers).

Information We Collect

When you call a business phone number that uses our service, and the call is not immediately answered, we may collect:

  • Your phone number
  • The content of text messages you send in response to our automated follow-up
  • Information you voluntarily provide during that text conversation (such as your name and what service you're interested in)

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Send a single automated follow-up text message after a missed call
  • Respond to your replies and help you schedule an appointment with the business you called
  • Provide the business with your inquiry so they can follow up with you directly
SMS Consent and Data Sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Text Messaging

By calling a business phone number that uses our service, you consent to receive one automated SMS follow-up if your call is not answered. Message frequency is limited to what's needed to respond to your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages.

Data Retention

We retain conversation data only as long as needed to fulfill the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, you can reach us through the business you originally contacted.
