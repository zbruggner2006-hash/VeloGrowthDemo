How it works

A customer calls the business phone number directly. If the call isn't answered right away, they receive one automated text message asking what they need and offering a link to schedule an appointment.

📞 Call Opt-In Notice

By calling a business phone number that uses the Velo Growth service (operated by Zach Bruggner), you consent to receive one automated SMS follow-up if your call isn't answered. This is explained verbally when applicable:

"Thank you for calling [Business Name]. If we're unable to reach you right away, you'll receive one automated text message to help you get scheduled. Message frequency varies based on your calls. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. By calling, you consent to receive this text."

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of future messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
